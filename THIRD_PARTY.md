# Third-party components

The Kotlin desktop application uses Kotlin, Compose Multiplatform, AndroidX,
Skiko, Skia, kotlinx.coroutines, JNA and a bundled OpenJDK runtime. Dependency
JARs retain their upstream license and notice files; the JDK includes its
legal directory. Refer to the resolved dependencies for the exact versions.

Qt is no longer a build or runtime dependency. The notices in licenses/Qt,
licenses/MinGW-Qt and licenses/SDK are retained for historical distributions.
Native SDK components retain their own licenses and are developed separately.
